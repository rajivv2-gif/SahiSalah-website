Frequently Asked Questions

Get immediate, transparent facts on MBA admissions and options.

Is it possible to complete an MBA in Germany without tuition fees?
Yes! State-funded German public universities charge zero tuition fees for both domestic and international students. You only need to cover your living costs (approx €934/month) which can be funded through scholarship programs or part-time student work.
Do I need a GMAT or CAT score for MBA admissions?
Top Tier-1 Indian institutes accept CAT or GMAT scores. European and UK universities primarily require GMAT scores or GRE equivalents, though some business schools accept profile-based submissions with substantial work experience.
What are the stay-back rights after studying an MBA abroad?
Germany offers an 18-month job seeker visa post-graduation, which seamlessly converts into a permanent residency (PR). The UK offers a 2-year post-study work (PSW) visa, and Australia offers up to 3 years depending on the location of study.
