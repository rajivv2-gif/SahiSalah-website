Frequently Asked Questions

Get immediate, transparent facts on PGDM admissions.

What is the main difference between PGDM and MBA?
PGDM (Post Graduate Diploma in Management) is offered by autonomous institutes approved by AICTE, whereas MBA (Master of Business Administration) is offered by university-affiliated colleges. PGDM programs tend to be more practical, industry-linked, and faster at updating their syllabus compared to university-bound MBAs.
Is a PGDM certificate valid for higher studies or government jobs?
Yes! If the PGDM program is approved by the AICTE and certified by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) as equivalent to an MBA, it holds the exact same status. It is fully valid for government job applications, public sector undertakings (PSUs), and pursuing Ph.D. programs globally.
What is a healthy ROI ratio for a PGDM program?
A healthy ROI ratio is when the audited average starting package equals or exceeds the total tuition fees (an ROI ratio of 1.0x or higher). For example, SPJIMR has a tuition fee of ~₹22.1L and an average package of ~₹33.0L, representing a high-yielding ROI of 1.49x.
