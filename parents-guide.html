Parent's Guide to Choosing the Right College

You have worked hard to finance your child's higher education. Avoid high-commission agent traps. Use verified data to evaluate safety, true ROI, and real prospects.

1. Return on Investment (ROI)

Higher fees do not always guarantee a better future. When calculating budget, parents must calculate the complete cost—including tuition, insurance, flight tickets, and living expenses—against real post-graduation salaries. For instance, public universities in Germany charge ₹0 tuition fees, saving parents ₹20L to ₹40L in student loans.

Positive Indicator Cost-saving study options (e.g. Germany Public Uni) with a payback period under 1.5 years.
Agent Trap Warning Agents pushing highly expensive private universities because they pay the agent up to 20% commission.

2. Real placements vs Fake Claims

Always request independently audited placement reports instead of relying on glossy brochures. Check the median salaries of actual graduates, average time taken to secure employment, and recruitment records of top MNCs. A university's placement rating should reflect real outcomes, not marketing promises.

Positive Indicator Transparent placement data, list of recruiting companies, and median salary figures.
Agent Trap Warning Claims of "100% placement guarantee" or inflated maximum salary packages that only 1 student out of 5,000 received.

3. Degree Recognition & Accreditation

Ensure the college is globally recognized and state-accredited. If your child intends to return to India to work or study further, the foreign degree must have Association of Indian Universities (AIU) equivalency. Unrecognized degrees can lead to rejection in public sector jobs or post-graduate admissions in India.

Positive Indicator Accreditation from recognized bodies (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS) and state-authorized status.
Agent Trap Warning Colleges offering "fast-track diplomas" that are unrecognized by national governments and local councils.

4. Student Safety & Local Support

Safety is the top concern for every parent sending their child away. Research student housing, campus security, local crime index, and healthcare access. In foreign countries, check if there is an active Indian student network. Having a reliable support system of seniors on campus ensures your child navigates initial struggles safely.

Positive Indicator On-campus or verified student housing options, robust health insurance, and active alumni clubs.
Agent Trap Warning Over-promising "cheap shared rooms" that are located in unsafe suburbs miles away from the main campus.

The Parent Guard: Sahi Salah connects your child directly with senior Indian students already studying on the target campus before they board their flight. Real peace of mind for parents.

5. Future prospects & Stay-Back Laws

Verify post-study work visa laws. Studying in countries that grant stay-back rights (e.g. 18 months in Germany, 2 years in the UK, 3 years in Canada for STEM) gives your child the necessary time to land an MNC job and earn back your education investment in local high-value currency.

Positive Indicator High demand local job market (STEM/Healthcare) and official post-study stay-back visa policies.
Agent Trap Warning Encouraging enrollment in countries or colleges with highly restrictive visa policies that force students back immediately after graduation.
